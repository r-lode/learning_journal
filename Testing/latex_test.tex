\subsection{A Little Math}

Consider the quadratic equation

\[
x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0.
\]

Factoring gives

\[
(x-2)(x-3)=0.
\]

Therefore,

\[
\boxed{x=2 \quad \text{or} \quad x=3}.
\]

\subsection{A Probability Example}

Suppose

\[
P(A)=0.40, \qquad P(B)=0.30,
\]

and

\[
P(A\cap B)=0.10.
\]

Then

\[
P(A\cup B)
=
P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)
=
0.40+0.30-0.10
=
\boxed{0.60}.
\]
